# Ransomware Incident Handler Journal

## Project Overview

This project documents a ransomware security incident affecting a small healthcare clinic. The incident disrupted business operations after employees were unable to access critical patient records and internal systems due to file encryption caused by ransomware.

The purpose of this journal entry is to analyze the incident, identify the attack vector, document the business impact, and recommend security improvements to reduce future risk.

---

## Incident Summary

A healthcare clinic experienced a ransomware attack after employees received targeted phishing emails containing malicious attachments. Once the attachment was downloaded, malware was installed on the affected systems, allowing attackers to deploy ransomware across the organization’s network.

The ransomware encrypted critical files, including patient records and operational data, resulting in significant disruption to business operations.

Employees reported being unable to access files, and ransom notes were displayed on infected systems demanding payment in exchange for a decryption key.

## The 5 W’s of the Incident

### Who caused the incident?

The incident was carried out by organized cybercriminal attackers who deployed ransomware through a phishing campaign.

---

### What happened?

Employees received phishing emails containing malicious attachments. After the attachment was downloaded, malware was installed on the employee systems, allowing attackers to gain unauthorized access and encrypt critical company files.

---

### When did the incident occur?

The incident occurred on a Tuesday morning at approximately 9:00 AM.

---

### Where did the incident happen?

The incident affected the healthcare clinic’s internal computer systems and employee workstations.

---

### Why did the incident happen?

The attack succeeded because employees interacted with phishing emails and downloaded malicious attachments. Insufficient phishing awareness and email security practices contributed to the compromise.

---

## Business Impact

* Employees lost access to critical patient data
* Business operations were disrupted
* Healthcare services experienced downtime
* Potential risk to sensitive medical information
* Financial and reputational damage risk

---

## Security Recommendations

* Conduct regular phishing awareness training
* Implement email filtering and attachment scanning
* Enforce multi-factor authentication (MFA)
* Maintain secure offline backups
* Regularly patch and update systems
* Deploy endpoint protection and monitoring tools
